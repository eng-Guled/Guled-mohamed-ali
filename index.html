A table for everyone

Asiya House Coffee started with one idea: food tastes better when it is shared. Our cooks prepare rice, meat and bread the way families do at home, and our tea is brewed slowly with whole spices.

Come for a quick breakfast, a long lunch with colleagues, or a family dinner. There is always room for one more.

  • Fresh daily Bread and sauces are made every morning.
  • Family tables Large platters for groups of 4 or more.
  • Halal All meat is halal.
